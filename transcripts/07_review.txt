     mapping is wrong, the stock math is wrong too.
  3. Usage can't show waste because waste isn't recorded. Usage is computed as
     recipe × POS. Nothing records waste (no waste table), and the M99 special
     has no recipe, so its ingredients aren't in the math at all. The 22 Sept
